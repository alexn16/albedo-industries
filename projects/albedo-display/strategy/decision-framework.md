## Direct answers
1. **Spend €1,000?** Yes, tranche it: €350 components, €250 enclosure/power tools, €200 user trials, €200 contingency; stop at failed gates.
2. **Prototype:** USB-powered 4.3-inch 480×272 TFT + ESP32-S3 dev board, rendering template/data payloads received from an Android BLE test harness.
3. **Buy first:** two different 4.3-inch SPI displays, ESP32-S3 dev kit, ESP32-C3 dev kit, USB-C supply/cables, logic analyser; buy duplicates only after compatibility.
4. **Prototype cost:** €120–€300 for one bench build; €500–€1,000 for two variants, fixtures and user-test enclosures. Planning estimate.
5. **Long-term BOM:** $15–22 is prudent before quotes; $10–15 remains a stretch target.
6. **Before €10k:** working demo; repeated use by 3/5 testers; one paid/conditional pilot; measured power/latency; three supplier responses; landed cost model.
7. **Before equity:** retention, clear payer, repeatable workflow, gross-margin route, compliance/tooling plan and founding execution capacity.
8. **Ten financing/strategic targets:** Seeed Studio, Espressif, Elecrow, HAX/SOSV, imec.istart, EIT Digital, CDTI, ENISA, XesGalicia/BIC Galicia ecosystem, relevant local angel after prototype. Verify current programmes before contact.
9. **Ten pilot/design-partner profiles:** independent hotel, coworking operator, small factory cell, clinic waiting area, vocational school, university lab, serviced apartment operator, multi-site retailer, logistics depot, accessibility organisation. Select named targets only after interviews.
10. **Most realistic first money:** paid design-partner prototype.
11. **Grants:** possible later; weak before technical novelty/R&D work package is clear.
12. **Accelerator:** realistic after working prototype and founder commitment; HAX-type manufacturing support fits better than generic acceleration.
13. **Crowdfunding:** potentially useful later, after reliable prototype, costed fulfilment and audience proof; a distraction now.
14. **Manufacturing vs VC:** supplier/ODM engineering and quotes are more useful now.
15. **Standalone potential:** possible only if a repeatable market and defensible software/distribution emerge.
16. **Structure now:** remain an independently operated Albedo Industries project.
