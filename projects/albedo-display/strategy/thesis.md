# Thesis

## Falsifiable claim
The phone/cloud can supply AI, internet, identity and input, leaving a powered BLE display endpoint simple enough to reach a **target, not confirmed**, US$10–15 volume BOM.

## Why it may work
Persistent information can remove the repeated unlock/open/search loop. A constrained endpoint may cost and age better than a tablet.

## Why it may fail
Cheap tablets and existing dashboards are capable; BLE dependence adds friction; commodity hardware offers little moat. Differentiation must emerge from one valuable workflow, software orchestration, fleet operations or distribution.
