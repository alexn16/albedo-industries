# Use-case hypotheses

| Hypothesis | Alternative | Why phone insufficient / tablet excessive | Payer | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Operations state | TV/KPI board/paper | needs point-of-work visibility; one state needs no tablet | operations lead | paid 10-device trial |
| Hospitality guest info | paper/tablet | changes per stay; tablet capability unused | operator | reduced staff questions/time |
| Personal next action | widget/smart display | phone hides amid distractions | consumer/employer | 3-week retention + price test |
| Classroom instruction | board/tablet | distributed calm instructions | school | teacher adoption + IT approval |
| Retail/service message | paper/ESL/signage | frequently changed small-format content | store operator | labour saving and robustness |
