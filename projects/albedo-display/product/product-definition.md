# Product definition

## V0 must have
4.3–5 inch dynamically updated display; BLE phone link; USB-C power; ESP32-class MCU; legible text, hierarchy, icons and simple graphics; explicit offline state.

## V0 must not have
Battery, touch, camera, microphone, speaker, cellular, local AI, expensive application processor or Wi-Fi dependency.

## Success
A user states intent (“show my next meeting and two priorities”), the phone produces a valid constrained payload, and the endpoint updates reliably.
