1. **Albedo Display** — ultra-low-cost physical interface for AI; concept status.
2. **Problem** — persistent information vs phone friction.
3. **Product** — V0 exclusions and user flow.
4. **Architecture thesis** — intelligence off-device; what must be proven.
5. **Prototype/evidence** — dated artefacts by status; no render ambiguity.
6. **Hardware economics** — ranges by evidence class, landed-cost gaps.
7. **Use cases** — three hypotheses and bottom-up placement logic.
8. **Business model** — paid pilot → B2B/fleet or OEM; alternatives.
9. **Validation roadmap** — gates, risks and kill criteria.
10. **Funding/milestones** — precise amount only when current plan/quotes exist.
