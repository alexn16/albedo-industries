# Crowdfunding assessment

Kickstarter and Indiegogo can test consumer willingness but reward polished campaigns and transfer fulfilment risk; Crowd Supply is more technically oriented. Comparable connected displays show that campaign funding does not prove sustainable demand or on-time delivery.

**Decision: potentially useful later; bad distraction now.** Preconditions: reliable EVT-like prototype, supplier quote, compliance/fulfilment plan, ≥30% contingency, demonstrated audience, realistic delivery date and no dependency on campaign funds for basic engineering. Record comparable campaign funding, backers, promised/actual delivery and complaints from campaign/update pages before launch.
