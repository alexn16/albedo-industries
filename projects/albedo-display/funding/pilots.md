# Paid pilot design

| Customer / decision maker | Problem/current solution | Pilot | Planning budget | Required proof |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| independent hotel / GM | repeated guest info; print/tablet | 10 rooms, 6 weeks | €1k–4k | setup, reduced questions, reliability |
| coworking / operations | room/desk state; tablets/signage | 10 endpoints | €1k–3k | integrations and update success |
| small factory / ops lead | cell instruction/KPI; paper/TV | 5 endpoints | €2k–6k | uptime, safety, labour impact |
| school / digital lead | distributed instruction; boards | 5 classrooms | €1k–3k | privacy, teacher use, IT approval |
| retail chain / store ops | changing small messages; paper | 20 endpoints | €2k–8k | labour saving and durability |

Budgets are hypotheses, not market evidence. A pilot counts only with money or equivalent procurement commitment, success metrics, owner and end date.
