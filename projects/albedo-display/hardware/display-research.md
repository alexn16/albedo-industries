# Display technology screen

| Technology | Dynamic layout | Power/refresh | Cost/lifecycle issue | V0 judgment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4.3-inch TFT module | colour, fast | continuous backlight | affordable retail modules; integrated controller cost | **prototype first** |
| Bare/OEM TFT | colour, fast | continuous | low unit potential; integration/MOQ risk | RFQ after demo |
| E-paper | excellent persistence | slow updates, low static power | 4–5 inch modules often costly | test only for calm use |
| Monochrome LCD | limited graphics | low | custom tooling/MOQ | not V0 |
| Surplus/recycled panel | capable | variable | traceability and lifecycle poor | experiment, not production |
| Industrial LCD | robust | continuous | cost premium | only for paid B2B need |

Required measurements: sunlight/angle readability, update latency, peak/idle power, controller RAM, cold boot, availability and lifecycle. Volume columns remain estimates until comparable supplier RFQs arrive.
