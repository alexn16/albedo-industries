# Outreach templates — do not send automatically

## Angel
I’m testing a deliberately narrow hardware thesis: keep AI on the phone and make the 4.3-inch BLE endpoint extremely simple. We have a costed prototype plan, not a finished company. Could I show you the evidence gates and get 20 minutes of pushback?

## VC
Albedo Display is pre-validation. Before considering a round, we’re testing one question: does phone-side AI enable a useful endpoint at materially lower cost than a tablet or smart display? Your work in [specific portfolio evidence] is why I’m writing. Open to a short critique?

## Hardware accelerator
We’re building a USB-powered 4.3-inch BLE display with no local AI. The immediate need is display/MCU integration and supplier validation, not generic mentoring. Does [programme] currently support this stage, and under what terms?

## Strategic manufacturer
We’re comparing a module prototype with a lower-cost integrated path. Could your team quote/sample a non-touch 4.3–5-inch display and explain MOQ, controller, lifecycle and 100/1k/10k pricing? We will not describe this as a partnership without agreement.

## Component supplier
Could you confirm availability, lifecycle, interface, brightness, MOQ, lead time and price breaks for [part]? The application is a low-frequency BLE information display; prototype quantity is [x].

## Pilot customer
You currently use [observed alternative] for [specific persistent information]. We’re testing a simpler screen, not selling a finished product. Would you help define a 4–6 week paid experiment with one metric and a clear stop decision?

## Grant body
We are assessing eligibility before shaping an application. Does [call] cover [specific technical work package], at our current TRL/entity stage? Please point us to the binding call text and co-financing rules.

## Design partner
We can prototype one persistent-information workflow around your real process. In return, we need weekly use data, candid setup feedback and a written buy/no-buy decision. Is that a fair exchange?
